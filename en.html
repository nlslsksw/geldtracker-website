Frequently asked questions

Is Geldtracker free?

Yes, the core features are completely free. For advanced features like groups and unlimited savings goals, there's Geldtracker Pro.

Can I try Pro first?

Yes. Geldtracker Pro comes with a 7-day free trial. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. You can manage or cancel anytime in your iOS settings.

Is the bank connection safe?

Bank sync is optional and off by default. It runs through a PSD2-licensed provider that only gets read access to your transactions. Neither we nor the app ever see your credentials.

What data is stored?

All data stays on your device. Only group data is synced via iCloud. We have no access to your financial data.

What devices does Geldtracker run on?

Geldtracker is available for iPhone and requires iOS 26.2 or later.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can create and export a complete backup as a JSON file at any time.

How do I cancel Pro?

You can cancel anytime through your iOS Settings under "Subscriptions". You keep access until the end of the billing period.
